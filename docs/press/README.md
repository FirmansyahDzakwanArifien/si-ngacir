## Deskripsi halaman (salin ke itch)

**Bahasa Indonesia**

> **Trobos Ajalah!** Masbro, capybara paling santai sekota, harus melunasi tunggakan sewa Kedai Chill, kedai minum dan kolam air hangat miliknya, dalam lima hari. Satu-satunya jalan: lolos masa percobaan sebagai kurir jalan kaki Ngacir Express.
>
> Seberangi jalan raya yang padat, naik rakit di sungai, hindari kucing jalanan, dan bertahan dari badai di jam sibuk. Kejar Timer ETA, tapi jaga paket tetap utuh: setiap benturan memotong upah, dan Bos Koko siap berteriak lewat earpiece. Di sisi lain, Kuro "El Gato" si kurir rival melesat dengan motor balapnya sambil membuang kulit pisang, dan Pak Buldog siap menggonggong di gang. Mau ikut ngebut, atau tetap santai?
>
> * 5 hari cerita dengan paket berbeda: gorengan untuk Nana yang tidak sabaran, kue ultah plus akuarium cupang, es krim yang cepat cair, laptop VIP di tengah badai, dan Patung Kristal Jeruk untuk Pak Wali Kota
> * Peta prosedural yang berubah setiap main: sungai dan rakit, koin, power-up, Pos checkpoint
> * Cutscene dialog dan malam di Kedai Chill setelah tiap pengiriman
> * Toko aksesori: topi, kacamata, dan tas untuk Masbro (jeruk di kepala sudah bawaan)
> * Setelah lulus: Shift Malam, main lagi hari mana pun untuk mengumpulkan koin
> * Main di browser, HP (swipe), atau Windows. Bahasa Indonesia dan English
> * Aksesibilitas: ukuran teks, mode buta warna, kurangi efek gerak
>
> **Kontrol:** WASD / panah (keyboard), swipe (HP). Esc / P untuk pause.

**English**

> **Just go for it!** Masbro, the most laid-back capybara in town, has five days to pay the overdue rent on Kedai Chill, his drink stall and warm pool. His only shot: pass a probation as an on-foot courier for Ngacir Express.
>
> Cross busy roads, hop on rafts across the river, dodge street cats, and survive a rush-hour storm. Beat the ETA timer, but keep the package intact: every bump cuts your pay, and head courier Boss Koko will yell at you through your earpiece. Meanwhile, rival courier Kuro "El Gato" zooms by on his racing moped dropping banana peels, and Pak Buldog the guard bulldog is ready to bark. Will you speed too, or stay chill?
>
> * 5 story days with different packages: hot fritters for impatient Nana, a birthday cake plus a betta tank, melting ice cream, a VIP laptop in a storm, and the Crystal Orange Statue for the Mayor
> * Procedural maps that change every run: rivers and rafts, coins, power-ups, checkpoints
> * Dialogue cutscenes and evenings at Kedai Chill after every delivery
> * Accessory shop: hats, glasses, and bags for Masbro (the orange on his head comes free)
> * After you pass: the Night Shift, replay any day to collect coins
> * Play in the browser, on your phone (swipe), or on Windows. English and Bahasa Indonesia
> * Accessibility: text size, color-blind modes, reduced motion
>
> **Controls:** WASD / arrow keys (keyboard), swipe (phone). Esc / P to pause.

## Deskripsi halaman (salin ke itch)

**Bahasa Indonesia**

> **Trobos Ajalah!** Masbro, capybara paling santai sekampung, harus mengumpulkan biaya pengobatan adiknya, Nana, dalam lima hari. Satu-satunya jalan: lolos masa percobaan sebagai kurir Ngacir Express.
>
> Seberangi jalan raya yang padat, naik rakit di sungai, hindari kucing jalanan, dan bertahan dari hujan deras di jam sibuk. Kejar Timer ETA, tapi jaga paket tetap utuh: setiap benturan memotong upah. Di sisi lain, Bang Kilat si kurir rival selalu ngebut demi rating. Mau ikut ngebut, atau tetap aman?
>
> * 5 hari cerita dengan paket berbeda: bantal, galon, telur Pak Ojan si perfeksionis, barang sensitif saat hujan, dan Paket VIP
> * Peta prosedural yang berubah setiap main: sungai dan rakit, koin, power-up, Pos checkpoint
> * Cutscene dialog dan malam di rumah bersama Nana setelah tiap pengiriman
> * Toko aksesori: topi, kacamata, dan tas untuk Masbro
> * Main di browser, HP (swipe), atau Windows. Bahasa Indonesia dan English
> * Aksesibilitas: ukuran teks, mode buta warna, kurangi efek gerak
>
> **Kontrol:** WASD / panah (keyboard), swipe (HP). Esc / P untuk pause.

**English**

> **Just go for it!** Masbro, the most laid-back capybara in the neighborhood, has five days to raise the money for his little sister Nana's treatment. His only shot: pass a probation as a courier for Ngacir Express.
>
> Cross busy roads, hop on rafts across the river, dodge street cats, and survive the rush-hour downpour. Beat the ETA timer, but keep the package intact: every bump cuts your pay. Meanwhile, rival courier Bang Kilat always speeds for ratings. Will you speed too, or stay safe?
>
> * 5 story days with different packages: a pillow, a water jug, picky Mr. Ojan's eggs, fragile goods in the rain, and the VIP package
> * Procedural maps that change every run: rivers and rafts, coins, power-ups, checkpoints
> * Dialogue cutscenes and evenings at home with Nana after every delivery
> * Accessory shop: hats, glasses, and bags for Masbro
> * Play in the browser, on your phone (swipe), or on Windows. English and Bahasa Indonesia
> * Accessibility: text size, color-blind modes, reduced motion
>
> **Controls:** WASD / arrow keys (keyboard), swipe (phone). Esc / P to pause.

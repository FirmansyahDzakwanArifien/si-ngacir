| --- | --- |
| **Masbro** (Broderick Capybarov) | The capybara who owns Kedai Chill, and the character you play. Ultra laid-back, deadpan, always carrying an orange on his head |
| **Boss Koko** (Bos Koko) | A cockatoo, head courier of Ngacir Express. Oversized shirt, red tie, giant clipboard. His crest stands up when he's angry. | **Nana** | An impatient VIP duck customer who always carries a stopwatch. Customer on Day 1 and Day 4 |
| **Kuro "El Gato"** | A black cat, senior courier at CepatKilat Corp. Tiny sunglasses, racing jacket, racing moped. Masbro's rival from Day 2 |
| **Pak Buldog** | A bulldog, the neighborhood guard, with a sarong around his neck and a night-watch cap. Hates people running around his neighborhood (Days 3–5) |
| **Landlord** (Pemilik Lahan) | A goat in a suit who owns the land under Kedai Chill and wants the overdue rent |
| **Mr. Mayor** (Pak Wali Kota) | A lion in batik with a sash. The most meticulous customer in town (Day 5) |

### Why a capybara?

Capybaras look cute and are loved by everyone from kids to young adults. In Indonesia they are also nicknamed "Masbro" because they look *chill* and calm in any situation. That contrast is exactly the point: the character is relaxed, but the player has to move quickly and carefully through traffic.

## Street folk

| NPC | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Drivers** | Honk "Tin tin!" when Masbro stands right in front of their vehicle |
| **Pedestrians** | Shoppers and a kid with a balloon walk slowly along the median. They don't damage the package, but they get in the way |
| **Meatball vendor** | A cart parked on the median that blocks your path and shouts "Bakso! Bakso!" |
| <img class="sprite" src="/img/sprites/cat_sit.svg" alt="" /> **Street cats** | Block a tile ("Meong!"). They don't damage the package |
| <img class="sprite" src="/img/sprites/vehicle_kuro.svg" alt="" /> **Kuro** | From Day 2, occasionally zooms through one of the lanes at high speed ("Move it, capybara!"). Hitting him still damages the package, and he sometimes drops a <img class="sprite" src="/img/sprites/item_kulit_pisang.svg" alt="" /> **banana peel** that makes Masbro slide one tile |
| <img class="sprite" src="/img/sprites/buldog_sit.svg" alt="" /> **Pak Buldog** | From Day 3, sits on a median. If Masbro passes within 1 tile, he barks ("GUK! GUK!") and Masbro panics |

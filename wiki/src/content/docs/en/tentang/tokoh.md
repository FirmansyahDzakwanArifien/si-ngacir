| Character | Who |
| --- | --- |
| **Masbro** | The most laid-back capybara in the neighborhood and the character you play. His rule: speed is fine, as long as the package arrives safe |
| **Nana** | Masbro's little sister, currently in hospital. Always waiting for Masbro to come home |
| **Bang Kilat** | A rival courier who loves speeding for ratings. Slowly learns that fast doesn't always win |
| **Bu Direktur** | The Director, owner of Ngacir Express, who judges Masbro's probation |
| **Bu Dokter** | The Doctor who treats Nana |
| **Pak Ojan** | The pickiest customer in town |

### Why a capybara?

Capybaras look cute and are loved by everyone from kids to young adults. In Indonesia they are also nicknamed "Masbro" because they look *chill* and calm in any situation. That contrast is exactly the point: the character is relaxed, but the player has to move quickly and carefully through traffic.

## Street folk

| NPC | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Drivers** | Honk "Tin tin!" when Masbro stands right in front of their vehicle |
| **Pedestrians** | Shoppers and a kid with a balloon walk slowly along the median. They don't damage the package, but they get in the way |
| **Meatball vendor** | A cart parked on the median that blocks your path and shouts "Bakso! Bakso!" |
| <img class="sprite" src="/img/sprites/cat_sit.svg" alt="" /> **Street cats** | Block a tile ("Meong!"). They don't damage the package |
| <img class="sprite" src="/img/sprites/vehicle_kilat.svg" alt="" /> **Bang Kilat** | From Day 3, occasionally zooms through one of the lanes at high speed. Hitting him still damages the package |

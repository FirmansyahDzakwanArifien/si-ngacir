> Masbro, a capybara who needs money for his little sister's medical treatment, must complete a five-day probation as a Ngacir Express courier. But time pressure, traffic, hazards, and the risk of damaging packages can cut his pay and threaten his chance to raise the money for her treatment.

## Plot

**Once upon a time**, there lived Masbro, a laid-back capybara who shared a home with his little sister, Nana, in Ngacir City.

**Every day**, Masbro made a living by relying on his calm, to provide for the two of them.

**One day**, Nana fell seriously ill and needed a huge hospital bill paid within five days.

**Because of that**, Masbro, out of options, applied to the delivery company Ngacir Express. He has to pass a five-day probation by delivering a string of risky packages: from a heavy water jug and fragile eggs to sensitive goods in the middle of the city's wild traffic.

**Because of that**, holding on to his motto *"Trobos Ajalah!"*, Masbro has to weave cleverly between vehicles to beat the ETA Timer, while protecting the package from bumps so his daily pay isn't cut by damage fines.

**Until finally**, if all goes well, Masbro delivers the VIP Package on the fifth day, is officially hired as a permanent courier, and raises enough money to cure his sister.

Masbro's fate is in your hands.

## Core conflict: fast or safe?

On the road, Masbro often runs into **Bang Kilat**, a rival courier who always speeds for ratings. Bang Kilat believes the fastest always wins. Masbro lives by a different rule: speed is fine, as long as the package arrives safe. Over the five days, both learn that speed doesn't always win.

## Story structure in the game

| Part | When it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Opening** | After choosing New Game, before Day 1 |
| **Night at home** | After Days 1–4. There are two versions: cheerful if the package was well protected, heavier if it took a lot of damage |
| **Morning** | Before Days 2–5, introducing that day's package and challenge |
| **Ending** | After Day 5, depending on whether the savings cover Nana's treatment |

Every cutscene you have already seen can be rewatched in the [Story Album](/en/fitur/album/).

## Endings

After Day 5, your total savings are compared with the treatment cost of **Rp350,000**.

- **Enough savings:** Masbro passes his probation, is hired as a permanent courier, and Nana gets her treatment.
- **Not enough:** a recap screen shows your pay for each day. Pick any day to replay and improve its pay.

After passing, the recap screen (main menu → **Continue**) still offers **Play Again** for every day, so you can keep collecting coins without starting a New Game.

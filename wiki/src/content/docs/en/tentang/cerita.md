> Masbro, the most laid-back capybara in town, has five days to pay the overdue rent on Kedai Chill. The only job that pays daily is a probationary courier position at Ngacir Express. But time pressure, traffic, hazards, and the risk of damaging packages can cut his pay and threaten his beloved stall.

## The world

**Ngacir City** is a crowded, noisy animal metropolis where everyone is in a hurry. Everyone speeds, everyone chases ratings.

In the middle of it sits **Kedai Chill**, Masbro's drink stall and warm pool, where the city's residents unwind after work.

**Ngacir Express** is the biggest courier service in town, with the motto *"Kalo gak kilat, gak selamat!"* ("If it ain't lightning, it ain't landing!"). Its couriers go **on foot**: in traffic this bad, a courier who dares to cross arrives sooner than a motorbike stuck in a jam. Pay is daily, with deductions for lateness or damaged packages.

## Plot

**Once upon a time**, there lived Masbro, the capybara who owns Kedai Chill and carries an orange on his head wherever he goes.

**Every day**, Masbro ran his stall at his own relaxed pace, never in a hurry.

**One day**, the Landlord showed up with a final warning letter (SP3): the overdue rent of **Rp350,000** must be paid within five days, or Kedai Chill gets cleared out.

**Because of that**, Masbro signed up as a probationary courier at Ngacir Express. Head courier Boss Koko hands him a string of risky packages: fritters that must stay crispy, a birthday cake with a betta fish tank, fast-melting ice cream, a VIP laptop in the middle of a storm, and finally a crystal statue for the Mayor.

**Because of that**, holding on to his motto *"Trobos Ajalah!"*, Masbro has to weave cleverly between vehicles to beat the ETA Timer and protect the package from bumps, all while dealing with rival courier Kuro and grumpy Pak Buldog.

**Until finally**, if all goes well, Masbro delivers the Crystal Orange Statue on the fifth day, is officially hired as a permanent courier, and the rent on Kedai Chill is paid.

The fate of Masbro and Kedai Chill is in your hands.

## Core conflict: speed or chill?

On the road, Masbro keeps getting overtaken by **Kuro "El Gato"**, a senior courier from CepatKilat Corp who zooms by on his racing moped and sometimes drops banana peels. Kuro believes the fastest always wins. Masbro, a courier on foot, lives by a different rule: go with the flow, but keep your brakes on. Speed is fine, as long as the package arrives safe.

## Story structure in the game

| Part | When it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Opening** | After choosing New Game, before Day 1: Kedai Chill, the Landlord's SP3, signing up at Ngacir Express, Kuro sneering |
| **Night at Kedai Chill** | After Days 1–4. There are two versions: cheerful if the package was well protected, heavier if it took a lot of damage |
| **Morning** | Before Days 2–5: Boss Koko's briefing, introducing that day's package and challenge |
| **Ending** | After Day 5, depending on whether the savings cover the rent |

During deliveries, Boss Koko also yells through your **earpiece** (a banner just below the top bar) when the package is nearly ruined or time is running out.

Every cutscene you have already seen can be rewatched in the [Story Album](/en/fitur/album/).

## Endings

After Day 5, your total savings are compared with the overdue rent of **Rp350,000**. The maximum pay over five days is Rp530,000 (excluding coins), so you don't have to be perfect, but you can't be sloppy either.

- **Enough savings:** the Mayor receives his statue, Boss Koko makes Masbro a permanent courier, Kuro admits defeat, and the rent is paid. Kedai Chill becomes the city couriers' rest stop.
- **Not enough:** the Landlord gives Masbro more time. A recap screen shows your pay for each day; pick any day to replay and improve its pay.

After passing, the recap screen (main menu → **Continue**) opens the **Night Shift**: **Play Again** for every day. Kedai Chill still has running costs, so you can keep collecting coins without starting a New Game.

## Rivers

From Day 2, some road blocks can be **rivers**. Hop onto a drifting **raft** or **wooden boat**; while standing on it, Masbro drifts with the current.

| Platform | Length |
| --- | --- |
| <img class="sprite" src="/img/sprites/vehicle_rakit3.svg" alt="" /> Raft | 2 or 3 tiles: one wooden panel per tile, split by rope lashings |
| <img class="sprite" src="/img/sprites/vehicle_perahu3.svg" alt="" /> Wooden boat | 2 or 3 tiles: one bench per tile |

- Jumping into the water without a raft, the raft drifting away, or being carried off-screen makes Masbro fall in ("BYUR!"): **the package gets wet** (1 damage) and Masbro returns to the last bank.
- Adjacent river lanes flow in opposite directions and are separated by a dashed foam line. Watch the raft in the next lane before you jump.
- Landing just past the end of a raft still counts; Masbro slides fully onto the raft.
- The helmet also protects against water.

## Checkpoints

From Day 2, long routes have a **checkpoint** (Pos) every few blocks: a clear median marked with flags on both sides. <img class="sprite" src="/img/sprites/checkpoint_flag_on.svg" alt="" /> As soon as Masbro steps on a checkpoint, its flags turn yellow.

If the package is destroyed **after** passing a checkpoint, you can choose **Continue from Checkpoint**: a replacement package is picked up and the trip resumes from the checkpoint. The trade-offs:

- A replacement fine of Rp15,000 (Rp20,000 for the Crystal Orange Statue) is deducted from your pay.
- Rating is capped at 3 stars.
- The clock keeps running.

## Boss Koko's earpiece

During deliveries, Boss Koko keeps an eye on you through your **earpiece**. His shouts appear as a banner just below the top bar, each once per day:

| Trigger | Shout |
| --- | --- |
| Package condition ≤ 34% | "HEY MASBRO! It's getting crushed! Ease up a little!" |
| ETA ≤ 15 seconds left | "Time's running out, Masbro! No daydreaming!" |

## Impact effects

Every event has a comic-style effect so it's clear what happened:

| Event | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Hit by a vehicle | **BRAK!**, Masbro spins, the screen freezes for a moment and flashes |
| Falling into the river | **BYUR!**, ripples and bubbles |
| Falling into a pothole | **GEDEBUK!** and dust |
| Helmet breaks | **TENG!** and shards |
| Package destroyed | **KRAK!** and smoke |

The flash and spin effects can be turned off with **Reduce motion** in [Settings](/en/fitur/pengaturan/).

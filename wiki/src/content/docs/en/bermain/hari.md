The levels follow a **progressive difficulty** principle: difficulty rises not only because there are more vehicles, but also because **the package you carry changes**.

## Day 1 — Introduction

The **pillow** package is soft and forgiving: it survives 4 hits. This day is for learning to move, cross, collide, and the pickup-and-delivery flow.

## Day 2 — Heavy package

The **water jug** makes Masbro step almost twice as slowly. Start crossing earlier. Day 2 always has a **river** with rafts, and street cats start to roam.

## Day 3 — Fragile package

**Eggs** take double damage: a single motorbike hit already wipes out 2 boxes. The customer is the perfectionist **Pak Ojan**: double damage fines and a maximum of 3 stars if the package has even a scratch. If the package is perfect and on time, Pak Ojan gives a **Rp25,000 tip**.

## Day 4 — Rain at rush hour

**Sensitive goods** in heavy rain. Only the area around Masbro is clearly visible, vehicles turn on their headlights, and the wet asphalt can make Masbro **slip** one extra step.

## Day 5 — VIP Package

The last day of the probation combines everything: the fastest traffic, rivers, cats, and light rain. The **VIP Package** is destroyed after just 2 damage. Choose your route and crossing timing carefully.

## Route length

Each day, the map is reassembled from road and river blocks separated by safe medians. Day 1 has 12 blocks (±45–55 rows, ETA about 75 seconds), and the route keeps getting longer up to Day 5 with 16 blocks (±75–90 rows, ETA about 135 seconds).

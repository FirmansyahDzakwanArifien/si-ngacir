## Day 1 — Fritters Must Stay Crispy

**Nana's** order of **hot fritters** can take a beating (it survives 4 hits), but it has to arrive before it goes cold and soggy. This day is for learning to move, cross, collide, and the pickup-and-delivery flow.

Nana is an **impatient customer**: double late deductions and a maximum of 3 stars if you're late. If the package is intact and on time, Nana gives a **Rp10,000 tip**.

## Day 2 — Steady Now, It's Fragile

A **birthday cake & betta tank** for **Bu Sri's** grandchild: a heavy package that makes Masbro step almost twice as slowly. Start crossing earlier. Day 2 always has the first **river** with rafts, street cats start to roam, and **Kuro** starts zooming through the lanes, dropping **banana peels**.

## Day 3 — The Alley Maze

The main road is closed for pipe works, so **Pak Budi's** **ice cream** has to go through the housing alleys. The tightest ETA and the biggest late deduction (Rp1,500 per second), asphalt full of **potholes**, and **Pak Buldog** starts guarding the medians.

## Day 4 — Storm at Rush Hour

**Nana's VIP laptop** for her meeting, in the middle of a storm. Only the area around Masbro is clearly visible, vehicles turn on their headlights, the wet asphalt can make Masbro **slip** one extra step, and rivers (floods) show up more often. Don't let the laptop fall in. Nana is still impatient; her tip is **Rp15,000** if it's perfect.

## Day 5 — Final Test: The Crystal Orange

The last day of the probation combines everything: the longest route, the fastest traffic, rivers, two cats, Pak Buldog, Kuro at his most frequent, and the tail end of the rain. The **Crystal Orange Statue** is destroyed after just 2 damage. The customer is the meticulous **Mr. Mayor**: double damage fines and a maximum of 3 stars if the package has even a scratch. If it's perfect and on time, he gives a **Rp25,000 tip**.

## Route length

Each day, the map is reassembled from road and river blocks separated by safe medians. Day 1 has 12 blocks (±45–55 rows, ETA about 75 seconds), and the route keeps getting longer up to Day 5 with 16 blocks (±75–90 rows, ETA about 135 seconds).

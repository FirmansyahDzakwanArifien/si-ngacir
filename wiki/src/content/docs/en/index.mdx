## What is Si Ngacir?

**Si Ngacir** is a 2D road-crossing arcade game. You play as **Masbro**, a capybara working as a courier for **Ngacir Express**. Pick up the package at the sender's stall, cross busy roads and fast-flowing rivers, and deliver it to the customer before time runs out.

The twist on the usual road-crossing game: **Masbro has no lives. What you protect is the package.** Every hit damages the package and cuts your pay.

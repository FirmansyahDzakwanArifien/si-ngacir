## Cutscenes

Si Ngacir's story is told through **dialogue cutscenes** with character portraits and illustrations. The music follows the mood: a sad theme with Nana at the hospital, a tense theme when Bang Kilat shows off, and relaxed music for everything else.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Enter / click / tap anywhere | Next page (or show the full text at once) |
| Esc / **Skip** button | Skips the cutscene |
| **Auto** button | Pages advance on their own once the text finishes. This choice is remembered |

## Previously…

When you press **Continue** on the main menu, the game plays a short **Previously…** recap: one sentence for each day you've completed plus your current savings. The recap can be skipped.

## Story Album

The **Story Album** on the main menu contains every cutscene you've already seen. Scenes not yet unlocked show as **???**. The album stays unlocked even if you choose New Game.

| Album contents | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Story Opening | 1 |
| Night of Days 1–4, smooth and heavy versions | 8 |
| Morning of Days 2–5 | 4 |
| Ending: Passed and Ending: Not Enough | 2 |
| **Total** | **15** |

:::tip
The *smooth* night version appears if the package condition is ≥ 70% on arrival; the *heavy* version if it's lower. To complete the album, try playing one day carefully and one day recklessly.
:::
